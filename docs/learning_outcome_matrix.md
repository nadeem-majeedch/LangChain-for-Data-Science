# Learning Outcome Matrix

## Course Learning Outcomes

By completing the LangChain for Data Science curriculum, students will be able to:

| LO | Learning Outcome | Reading | Notebook | Assessment | Quiz | Capstone |
|----|-----------------|---------|----------|------------|------|----------|
| 1 | Explain LLM application architecture and how LangChain fits into the stack | 01 | 01 | 01 | 01 | 17 |
| 2 | Configure and use language models via cloud APIs and local Ollama | 01, 02 | 01, 02 | 01, 02 | 01, 02 | 17 |
| 3 | Design reusable prompt templates and message systems | 02 | 02 | 02 | 02 | 17 |
| 4 | Build composable chains using LCEL (LangChain Expression Language) | 03 | 03 | 03 | 03 | 17 |
| 5 | Generate and use embeddings for semantic search | 04 | 04 | 04 | 04 | 17 |
| 6 | Build vector-store-based retrieval systems | 04, 05 | 04, 05 | 04, 05 | 04, 05 | 17 |
| 7 | Implement RAG applications that answer questions over custom knowledge bases | 05, 08 | 05, 08 | 05, 08 | 05, 08 | 17 |
| 8 | Create safe, validated tools for LLM applications | 06 | 06 | 06 | 06 | 17 |
| 9 | Build agents that reason and select appropriate tools | 06, 11 | 06, 11 | 06, 11 | 06, 11 | 17 |
| 10 | Build stateful workflows using LangGraph | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 17 |
| 11 | Evaluate LLM applications using automated and LLM-as-judge methods | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 17 |
| 12 | Identify and mitigate LLM security risks (prompt injection, tool abuse, data leakage) | 14 | 14 | 14 | 14 | 17 |
| 13 | Build safe natural-language SQL interfaces | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 17 |
| 14 | Understand and use the Model Context Protocol (MCP) | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 17 |
| 15 | Design production-oriented LLM systems with error handling, caching, and monitoring | 16 | 16 | 16 | 16 | 17 |
| 16 | Build a complete Data Science AI Copilot combining all techniques | 01–17 | 01–17 | 17 | Final | 17 |
